A note on "FHD" and 1920 × 1280

The listing uses the term FHD alongside a resolution of 1920 × 1280. Standard Full HD is 1920 × 1080, so this is not the same thing. 1920 × 1280 works out to a 3:2 aspect ratio (our calculation from the listed resolution), which is taller than the 16:9 shape of most video and monitors. Expect narrow black bars when watching 16:9 video.

Display features, in plain terms

1920 × 1280 resolution

Roughly 2.5 million pixels on a 10.5-inch panel, which should give fine detail for text at this size.

100% sRGB

sRGB is the standard colour range for most web content and everyday apps. The listing advertises full coverage of it.

76% DCI-P3

DCI-P3 is a wider range used in cinema and some devices. 76% means it covers part, not all, of that range.

420 nits brightness

A measure of peak light output. Suitable for typical indoor lighting; bright sunlight will be harder.

Honest advantages and limitations

Advantages

  • Compact 10.5-inch size is easy to carry and place.
  • Advertised 100% sRGB coverage suits everyday content.
  • Higher-than-1080p pixel count for the screen size, as advertised.
  • Gives you a second screen without a full-size monitor.

Limitations

  • A small screen: not a replacement for a full-size monitor.
  • 1920 × 1280 is 3:2, not 16:9, so video will show bars.
  • 76% DCI-P3 is not a wide-gamut display; not aimed at professional colour grading.
  • 420 nits may be dim in bright daylight.
  • Ports, speakers, touch support, battery and warranty are not covered here; check the listing.
