How mini-app rewards can work

Different apps use different reward systems. A displayed point or token balance does not necessarily mean you can withdraw money. Always verify the rules inside the specific app.

🎮

Gameplay rewards

Some apps award points for tapping, playing, completing levels, or checking in. Points may only be usable inside the app and may have no cash value.

🪙

Points and tokens

Some projects display points or digital tokens. A token's future value, exchange availability, and ability to be sold are not guaranteed.

📋

Eligibility and payouts

Reward programmes may have location limits, minimum thresholds, deadlines, identity requirements, or other conditions. Read the app's official terms.

Before trying an app: a quick checklist

Use these checks for each app rather than assuming all mini-apps work the same way.

  • Find the app's official terms and privacy policy, if available.
  • Confirm what a reward represents and whether it can be withdrawn.
  • Check payout thresholds, fees, eligibility, and country restrictions.
  • Never assume a reward amount or token price will increase.
  • Be cautious if an app asks for an upfront payment to unlock a payout.
  • Never share passwords, login codes, or wallet recovery phrases.

How to evaluate a mini-app

1. Identify the operator

Check who runs the app and whether the project's official information is consistent.

2. Read the conditions

Look for eligibility rules, payout details, fees, data collection, and restrictions.

3. Decide for yourself

Only participate if you understand the conditions and are comfortable with the risks.
